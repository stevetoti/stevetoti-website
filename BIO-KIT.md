## Usage notes

- **Lead with the award** on any profile where credibility matters most
  (LinkedIn, speaking applications, proposals). It is the strongest single
  differentiator and it is verifiable.
- **Lead with the local-language AI** for press, community and African/Pacific
  audiences — it is the most distinctive part of the story, not the automation
  services, which many people also sell.
- **Lead with the Medd Australia / Connect The Docs role** for technical and
  enterprise audiences: it proves you ship production AI, not just advise.
- Keep emoji use to the short-form platforms; leave it out of press and
  third-person bios.
- Rotate the X/Twitter and Instagram lines when a new award, product or
  market lands — short bios date fastest.
